# Documentation

that works and how to add to it.

  flakes are not inputs
- [Unification](./unification.md) — which inputs are substituted, and how to
  choose your own policy
- [Adding or removing a flake](./adding-a-flake.md) — `manual.txt`,
  `blocklist.txt`, and why names never change
- [Building the index](./building-the-index.md) — the tools, the databases,
  and what pinning costs
- [Caveats](./caveats.md) — read before depending on this
